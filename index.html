About the actor

Celebrating Theo James

Theo James is an English actor known for roles across film and television, including the Divergent film series and the television series The White Lotus and Sanditon.

This independent fan club is a place to share appreciation for his work, discuss performances and keep up with publicly announced projects.

Fan-site notice: This website is independently created by fans. It is not official, is not managed by Theo James or his representatives, and does not imply his endorsement.
